# Supply Chain Performance Dashboard (MIS)

### Project Overview
This dashboard was developed to provide actionable insights into supply chain operations, focusing on production efficiency, quality control, and cost management. As an MIS professional with 3.5 years of experience, I designed this tool to monitor key metrics that directly impact operational success.

### Key Metrics Tracked
* *Total Shipping Cost:* Analyzed at *$83.48K* to monitor logistics expenditure.
* *Total Defect Rate:* Maintained at *2.87%*, supporting quality assurance goals.
* *Average Lead Time:* Monitored at *4.74 days* to ensure timely delivery and supply chain agility.

### Technical Skills Demonstrated
* *Advanced Power BI:* Used for high-level data visualization and reporting.
* *Complex DAX:* Implemented functions for running totals, Year-over-Year (YoY) analysis, and custom KPI calculations.
* *SQL Data Extraction:* Leveraged SQL to clean and transform raw datasets for accurate reporting.
* *Data Modeling:* Structured multiple data tables (Defective Units, Lead Time, Shipping Costs) into a cohesive star schema.

### Business Impact
By identifying top suppliers with high defect rates and monitoring shipping cost trends, this dashboard facilitates data-driven decisions that help in reducing wastage and optimizing lead times.
